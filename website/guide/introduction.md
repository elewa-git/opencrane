# What is OpenCrane?

OpenCrane is building a **shared company workspace for AI assistance**. Its goal is to give each
employee a private assistant, connect that assistant to useful company tools and knowledge, and
let teams automate repeatable work with shared agents.

## The five parts of the product

| Concept | What it means |
|---|---|
| People and teams | The members of your organisation and the groups used to organise access. |
| Assistants | A personal assistant helps one person. A shared agent is configured for a company task with its own permissions. |
| Conversations | The place where people ask for work, follow it and return to its history. Group conversations are chats; company access groups organise permissions. |
| Tools, knowledge and files | Tools act in other systems, knowledge provides context, and files hold inputs and results. Memory is useful context retained between conversations. |
| Company controls | Administrators choose models, grant access, set limits and review activity. |

An assistant is who helps you; a conversation holds a particular piece of work. You should not
need to understand how its computer is started or replaced to use either.

## A useful outcome

Imagine asking your assistant to prepare for a customer meeting. The intended experience is that it
can find the company information it may read, draft a brief, ask before an action that needs
approval, and keep the result with the conversation. A shared agent could later prepare the same
kind of brief on a schedule for a team.

This is the product vision. The [development status](/guide/status) separates the pieces already
built from the task journeys still to complete.

## What your organisation controls

Your organisation operates the OpenCrane installation and chooses its identity provider, models,
integrations and storage. Personal work stays restricted to its authorised participants, and a
shared agent must be given the access its task needs.

Using your own installation does not make an external model provider local. The models and
integrations your organisation chooses can receive the data needed for their authorised requests.
